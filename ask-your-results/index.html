    How it works

    Member questionIn the app, next to the results. Urgent symptoms are caught first and get a fixed safety message, no AI
    Grounded modelSees only this member's results, protocol and a reviewed reference library. Must name the markers and notes it used
    Number checkEvery number in the answer is matched against the results. A mismatch triggers one rewrite, then it is flagged
    Scope rulesNo diagnosis, no starting, stopping or changing doses. Those questions get an explanation and a route to the clinician
    Clinician queueAny answer about an out-of-range marker is flagged for review, in code, not left to the model
    Fewer “what does this mean?” ticketsMembers get answers about their own numbers when they open the report, not days later, and clinicians see only the questions that need them.
    A reason to come back for the retestThe baseline-to-retest comparison is the story of the membership. Asking about it makes the progress concrete.
    Trust you can auditEvery answer carries its sources and checks, so the clinical team can review what the assistant said and why.
