Closed Loop 1 · Fuel + 1, a phygital non-fuel retail demo (Meridian Stations is fictional)

Recognise the car, offer one thing worth having, and prepare it while the customer is fuelling.

Most drivers fuel and leave without ever entering the shop. Here the pump greets them, AI picks one offer during the four minutes they stand there, a runner brings it to the car, and a holdout group shows what it really earned. It pairs with Closed Loop, which fixes what customers complain about.

The visit

Meridian Al Reem, pump 4. Change the driver or the conditions and every step adapts.
1
Customer · arrive

The pump knows the car, so it greets the driver.

A camera reads the plate and matches it to a registered vehicle in the app. The public screen says hello and nothing more. The name appears only if the driver opted in, and offers, points and the car stay on their phone.

2
AI engine · decide

Pick one offer worth interrupting for, or none.

Every possible offer is scored on how likely this driver is to take it right now, what it earns after cost, and how much is lost on people who would have paid full price anyway.

Guardrails remove anything that can't reach the car before fuelling ends, is out of stock, or falls below the margin floor. Then a language model writes the words, in the driver's language.

    Customer · fuel and add

    One tap while fuelling. It's at the window before the nozzle clicks off.

    The driver pays for fuel with Mobile Pay. While the tank fills, one offer appears. They can pay with the same payment or with points they have never used. The success screen carries the one thing that can wait, like a service booking.

    Try it: press Start fuelling. Four minutes run in about fifteen seconds.

    Station team · deliver

    The shop prepares it, and a runner walks it to pump 4.

    The order lands on the shop screen and the forecourt runner's handheld with the pump, the car's colour and the masked plate. The AI only offered it because prep, queue and walking time fit inside the fuelling time.

    If it runs late anyway, the promise flips: it waits at the counter and the driver gets points back automatically.

    5
    AI engine · measure honestly

    Did it earn money, or just discount sales that would have happened anyway?

    One in ten app-paying drivers never sees an offer. Comparing everyone else with that holdout group separates new sales from people who would have walked in and paid full price.

    A 4-week pilot at 10 stations, about 150 app-paid fuel visits a day each. Move the sliders to see when it stops paying.

    Station team · beyond one visit

    Rules that keep it welcome, and what it's worth across a network

    Hello in public, everything else in privateThe pump shows a first name only with opt-in. Offers, points and the car stay on the driver's phone.
    One offer, or noneIf nothing beats the margin floor and the chance of a yes, the driver just fuels in peace.
    Never promise what the station can't doPrep time, counter queue and stock are checked live before anything is offered.
    Respect a "no thanks"The same item isn't offered again for 7 days, and each station has a daily discount budget.
    Runners stay safeMarked walking lanes only, never between moving cars. The car stays put until the nozzle is back.
    Always keep a holdout10% of visits get no offer, so the value is measured, not claimed.
