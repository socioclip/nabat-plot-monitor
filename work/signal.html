The problem

Why this needed to exist

Every product team has thousands of reviews, tickets and survey answers, and almost nobody reads them. The feedback that could change the roadmap sits in a spreadsheet while decisions get made on the loudest opinion in the room.

I have lived this problem as a PM. Signal is the tool I wanted.

The product

How it works

  1. Paste reviews, support tickets or open survey answers in any format.
  2. The model clusters them into themes and scores sentiment for each theme.
  3. It proposes concrete actions, each with estimated impact, effort and a success metric.
  4. The result reads like the first draft of a prioritisation discussion, not a word cloud.
Product decisions

The calls that mattered

Actions, not just insights

A sentiment chart doesn't change a roadmap. An action with impact, effort and a metric attached can go straight into a backlog review.

Themes grounded in counts

Each theme shows how many pieces of feedback sit behind it, so a vivid but rare complaint doesn't outrank a common one.

Zero setup

No integrations or tagging taxonomy to maintain. Paste and go, so it gets used.

Measuring success

What I'd measure

This is a prototype, so these are the metrics I'd use to judge it in production, not results I'm claiming.
