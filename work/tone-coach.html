The problem

Why this needed to exist

In travel and payments, most escalations aren't caused by the original problem. They're caused by the reply. A correct but cold answer to an angry customer turns a refund into a complaint, and a complaint into a churned customer.

Agents under pressure don't have time to re-read their own drafts through the customer's eyes.

The product

How it works

  1. Paste the customer's message. Tone Coach detects the tone and the emotions behind it, with a confidence score for each.
  2. Draft your reply as you normally would.
  3. The model checks how your draft is likely to land given the customer's mood, and rewrites it to be polite, warm and respectful while keeping the facts.
  4. An Evals view tests the model against a fixed set of sample messages, so prompt changes can be checked before they reach agents.
Product decisions

The calls that mattered

Two steps, not one

Reading the customer first and checking the reply second mirrors how good agents already think, so it fits the workflow instead of replacing it.

Keep the agent in control

The tool suggests a rewrite. It never sends. Accountability stays with the person.

Evals built in

Tone is subjective, so a fixed eval set is the only way to know if a change made things better or just different.

Measuring success

What I'd measure

This is a prototype, so these are the metrics I'd use to judge it in production, not results I'm claiming.
