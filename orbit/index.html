Product notes

Built for the analyst, not the data scientist

Satellite data is powerful but hard to use. Most people who need answers from it (a port operations lead, a project owner, a policy team) can’t write a query or read a spectral band. Orbit tests one idea: let them ask in plain language and get a note they can trust and act on.

Who it’s for

  • Port and logistics teams checking congestion and yard use
  • Project owners tracking construction progress against plan
  • Analysts who need a first read before ordering fresh imagery

The calls that mattered

  • Change is the signal. Two dates by default, because “what’s different” is usually the real question.
  • A note, not a chat. Every answer has the same structure, so it’s quick to scan and compare.
  • Confidence and limits are mandatory. Counts are ranges. The model must say what the imagery can’t show.
  • Honest about the data. Real capture dates and sources on every image; archive imagery, labelled as such.

What I’d build next

  • Radar (SAR) imagery so night and cloud don’t block the answer
  • Watch a site and get an alert when something changes
  • Object detection to back counts with marked boxes, not just estimates
  • One-click export of the note as a signed brief
