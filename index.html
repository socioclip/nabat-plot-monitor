Approach

How I build AI products

Models change every few months. These principles don't, and they're what separate a demo from a product people trust with real work.

i.

Start from the decision

Who has to decide what, and what would change their mind? The product is whatever gets them there faster and with more confidence.

ii.

Ground everything

In high-stakes work an answer is only as good as its evidence. Show the source, show the quote, and say what isn't known.

iii.

Evals before launch

Define what "good" looks like with a fixed test set before tuning anything. It turns opinions about quality into a number.

iv.

Prototype it myself

I build the first version so engineers and users react to something real. The conversation moves from "what if" to "what next".
