1
Developer · tender issued

One Bill of Quantities goes out to four contractors.

The quantity surveyor measures the works into a BOQ: every item, its unit and quantity. Contractors fill in rates. Provisional sums are fixed amounts everyone must carry unchanged.

2
Contractors · bids received

Bids come back, each in its own format.

Different wording, different units, lines in a different order, a missing rate, notes at the bottom. This is exactly what the contractor sent. Pick a bidder to see their sheet.

3
AI copilot · mapping

Match every bid line to the tender BOQ.

The copilot reads each description, expands trade shorthand (“Blk”, “F/W”, “Reinf.”), compares sizes and grades, checks the unit is compatible, and converts units where needed.

Low-confidence matches go to the QS for a one-click check instead of being guessed.

4
AI copilot · checks

Catch errors, gaps and fine print.

Every bid is checked for arithmetic slips, items left unpriced, quantities changed from the tender, provisional sums altered, and qualifications hidden in the notes. Each finding carries its money impact.

5
AI copilot · should-cost

Check every rate against what it should cost.

Each rate is compared with a benchmark range from past tenders. Red rates are suspiciously low, a risk of claims later. Amber rates are high, so they are worth negotiating.

The copilot also checks whether a bidder has front-loaded the price into early work to get paid sooner.

6
AI copilot · levelling

Level the bids so they compare like for like.

Starting from each submitted total, the copilot corrects arithmetic, re-prices tender quantities, adds a price for missing items, restores provisional sums, and adds allowances for risky qualifications and rates priced below the realistic range.

Change the rules to see the ranking move.

7
Developer · award

Recommend a contractor and what to negotiate.

The cheapest bid on paper is often not the cheapest bid. The copilot recommends on the levelled price, lists the rates worth negotiating, and drafts the clarification questions for each bidder.
