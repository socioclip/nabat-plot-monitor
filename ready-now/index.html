AI engine · reach

Find everyone within 20 minutes of the rider.

Reach is measured in road time from where the rider is right now, not as a circle on the map. Expressways stretch the zone; side streets shrink it.

Then safety and fairness rules remove anyone who shouldn’t get it.

AI engine · pricing

Choose the discount that earns the most.

A bigger discount makes a rescue more likely but earns less. The engine tries every level from 10% to 40% and picks the peak of the orange line.

5
AI engine · notifications

Send the offer in small waves, best matches first.

Five people at a time, every 90 seconds, until someone claims it or the food-quality window closes. Small waves keep it personal and stop people learning to wait for cancelled food.

Business · beyond this one order

Rules that keep it fair, and what it’s worth at scale

No buying back your own orderThe cancelling account, address and devices are excluded, so no one can cancel and reclaim it cheaper.
Weekly claim capThree rescues per customer per week, so it stays a pleasant surprise rather than a way to shop.
Repeat cancellers pay moreThe fee steps up for accounts that often cancel after dispatch.
No cannibalisationAnyone with their own order in progress is skipped.
Chilled food never travels twiceIce cream, shakes and smoothies can’t be cancelled once on the road.
