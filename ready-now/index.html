Let customers cancel after dispatch, and get the meal to someone nearby who wants it.

Price it: the discount that earns the most

For every discount level the model re-scores everyone in reach, works out the chance someone claims it within the food-quality window, and adds what the platform earns: cancel fee + delivery fee − discount − rider's extra leg, plus the value of acquiring a new customer.

5

Offer it in waves, not a broadcast

Five customers per wave, best first, every 90 seconds. Small waves keep the offer feeling personal and stop people learning to wait for cancelled food.

What the new customer sees

Name it for what it is: ready food, nearby"Ready Now" sells speed and freshness. The word "cancelled" never appears in the offer.
Be open about whyOne plain line explains the plans changed after it left the kitchen. Hiding it would backfire the first time someone found out.
Proof it was never openedKitchen seal time, tamper-evident sticker, and the rider's photo of the seal at pickup.
Only send what they'd order anywayOffers go to people whose order history matches the cuisine. Vegetarians only ever see vegetarian orders.

Guardrails against gaming

No buying back your own orderThe cancelling account, its address and its devices are excluded, so no one can cancel and reclaim at a discount.
Weekly claim capThree rescues per customer per week, so it stays a pleasant surprise rather than a way to shop.
Repeat cancellers pay moreThe cancel fee steps up for accounts that cancel often after dispatch.
No cannibalisationCustomers with their own order in progress, or who opened the app to order in the last 10 minutes, are skipped.
Restaurant is paid as normalThe kitchen receives its usual payout whether or not the meal is rescued.

What it's worth at scale

Assumptions are editable placeholders, to be replaced with the platform's own data. For reference, a large Indian food delivery app reported that about three in four eligible cancelled orders were being claimed after it launched a similar feature. Average margin per rescue comes from step 4.
