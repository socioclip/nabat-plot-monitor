Find the bookings that don’t match. Fix them before the guest arrives.

Every hotel booking exists three times: in the buyer’s system, at the hotel, and in payments. When they disagree, a guest arrives to no room or money goes missing. This desk finds those bookings, an AI agent explains each one and drafts the fix, and a person approves it.

How it works

Finding is plain SQL, not AI. Whether two records disagree is a fact, so four SQL checks run on a real SQLite database in your browser. The rules include a 1% tolerance for rounding and a 30-minute grace period for bookings the hotel hasn’t answered yet, so ops only sees real problems.

Two AI agents, limited permissions. The Investigator can only read the booking’s data and explains why the records disagree. The Resolver can only draft one message. Neither can send, cancel or refund anything. A person approves every draft.

Fictional company, hotels, buyers, guests and figures. SQL runs on SQLite in your browser (sql.js, MIT licence). The agents write with an AI model through this site’s server; if it is unavailable they fall back to rule-based text and say so. Built by Syed Sarfaraz Ahmed.
